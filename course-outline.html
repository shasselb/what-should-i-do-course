What Should I Do?

A Practical Guide to God-Honoring Decisions.

We have all asked and answered “What should I do?” thousands of times each day. We all want to make great decisions, but something happens between our good intentions and the choices we actually make.

In this course, you will study great decision makers like Abraham, David, and Peter. You will learn a practical process for making God-honoring decisions that will help you live a transformed life.

WHAT YOU WILL LEARN

Learning Objectives

  1. 01

    BE

    Be who God created you to be.

    Be confident in your identity in Christ, so that you stand firmly and make decisions that honor God.

  2. 02

    KNOW

    Know God. Know what has eternal value.

    When you align your values with what has eternal value, then your decisions will honor God.
