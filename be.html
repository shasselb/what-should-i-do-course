01

BE

Be who God created you to be.

Be confident in your identity in Christ, so that you stand firmly and make decisions that honor God.

FACILITATION OUTLINE

Guide the BE conversation

  1. 01

    Set the focus

    Invite participants to consider how identity shapes everyday decisions.

    +

    Facilitator prompt

    Ask participants to name some of the labels people use to define themselves. Discuss which labels can change and which identity remains secure.

    Discussion questions

    • Where do people commonly look for identity?
    • How can an unstable identity affect a difficult decision?
    • What changes when identity begins with belonging to Christ?

    Key idea: We make healthier decisions when we begin with who we are in Christ.

  2. 02

    Discuss

    What does it mean to find your identity in Christ rather than in circumstances, roles, or achievements?

    +

    Explore together

    Compare identity rooted in Christ with identity rooted in performance, approval, possessions, relationships, or success.

    Discussion questions

    • Which temporary source of identity is most tempting for you?
    • How does fear of failure influence decisions?
    • What truth about your identity in Christ do you need to remember?

    Listen for: Freedom from earning worth, greater courage, and decisions shaped by faith rather than fear.

  3. 03

    Reflect

    Where do you feel confident in who God created you to be? Where do you need greater confidence?

    +

    Personal reflection

    Allow a few quiet minutes for participants to consider their strengths, calling, relationships, and current season.

    Reflection questions

    • Where have you seen God shaping you?
    • What insecurity most often affects your choices?
    • What would standing firmly look like in that area?

    Optional sharing: Invite each person to share one truth they want to carry forward.

  4. 04

    Put faith into action

    Name one decision you can approach this week from a secure identity in Christ.

    +

    Choose a decision

    Ask each participant to identify one real decision where fear, approval, or uncertainty has been influential.

    Action questions

    • What choice are you facing?
    • What would honor God in this situation?
    • What is one faithful next step, and when will you take it?

    Close in prayer: Ask God for confidence to act from a secure identity in Christ.
