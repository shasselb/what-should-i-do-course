HOW IT WORKS Complete the values exercise in five steps
  1. 01 Sort every card

    Place each value in Yes, No, or Maybe.

  2. 02 Review your Yes pile

    Group related words and move cards as your thinking changes.

  3. 03 Narrow to 3–5 values

    Keep only the words that are most meaningful to you.

  4. 04 Check biblical alignment

    Choose values that reflect Scripture and your identity in Christ.

  5. 05 Make them actionable

    Finalize your list, then add an action verb to each value on the My Values page.

Your outcome: 3–5 biblically aligned commitments, such as Cultivate Wisdom or Practice Patience.
