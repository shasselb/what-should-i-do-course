03

DO

Do only what honors God.

Build habits that reflect your identity in Christ.

FACILITATION OUTLINE

Guide the DO conversation

  1. 01

    Set the focus

    Connect repeated daily actions with the identity and values explored in BE and KNOW.

    +

    Facilitator prompt

    Explain that habits turn repeated decisions into patterns. Those patterns either reinforce or compete with our identity and values.

    Discussion questions

    • How do small repeated choices shape a life?
    • What makes a habit difficult to notice?
    • How are BE, KNOW, and DO connected?

    Key idea: Faith moves into action through consistent, ordinary choices.

  2. 02

    Discuss

    Which habits currently reflect your identity in Christ? Which habits compete with it?

    +

    Explore current habits

    Consider routines involving Scripture, prayer, work, relationships, rest, health, technology, money, and service.

    Discussion questions

    • Which habit helps you live faithfully?
    • Which habit regularly pulls you away from what you value?
    • What situation or emotion tends to trigger that habit?

    Facilitator note: Encourage honesty without shame; the purpose is awareness and growth.

  3. 03

    Review the evidence

    Complete the Time Audit and look for patterns in where attention, energy, and time are going.

    +

    Read the patterns

    Review the month as evidence rather than as a scorecard. Look for repeated choices, competing priorities, and opportunities to realign time.

    Debrief questions

    • Where did most of your discretionary time go?
    • What pattern surprised or concerned you?
    • Where does your schedule already reflect what God values?

    Look deeper: Ask what the pattern reveals about priorities, energy, boundaries, and environment.

  4. 04

    Choose a next action

    Identify one habit to begin, strengthen, reduce, or stop so your daily decisions better honor God.

    +

    Build a practical plan

    Keep the next step specific and realistic. Connect it to a time, place, trigger, and source of support.

    Action questions

    • What exactly will you do differently?
    • When and where will this action happen?
    • What obstacle should you prepare for?
    • Who can encourage or hold you accountable?

    Commitment: Complete the sentence, “This week, I will honor God by…”
