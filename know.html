02

KNOW

Know God. Know what has eternal value.

When you align your values with what has eternal value, then your decisions will honor God.

FACILITATION OUTLINE

Guide the KNOW conversation

  1. 01

    Set the focus

    Explain that what people value influences how they evaluate options and make decisions.

    +

    Facilitator prompt

    Introduce values as the priorities and convictions that help us decide what deserves attention, time, and action.

    Discussion questions

    • How can you recognize what a person truly values?
    • When have two important values competed in a decision?
    • Why is it important to examine our values intentionally?

    Key idea: Our decisions reveal what we value, even when we have not named those values.

  2. 02

    Discuss

    What has eternal value? How can knowing God reshape what matters most to us?

    +

    Explore together

    Contrast priorities that are temporary with those that reflect God’s purposes and endure beyond present circumstances.

    Discussion questions

    • What does Scripture show us about what God values?
    • Which cultural priorities can distract us from eternal value?
    • How does knowing God change what we pursue?

    Listen for: Love of God and neighbor, faithfulness, truth, character, service, and spiritual growth.

  3. 03

    Complete the exercise

    Sort the value cards into Yes, No, and Maybe, then review and confirm the strongest values.

    +

    Exercise guidance

    Encourage honest first reactions. Participants can move cards between piles while they clarify what matters most.

    Debrief questions

    • Which value was easiest to place? Which was hardest?
    • What surprised you about your Yes pile?
    • Which values need to be tested against what God values?

    Facilitator note: The goal is thoughtful alignment, not comparison between participants.

  4. 04

    Apply

    Choose one confirmed value and describe how it should guide a decision you are currently facing.

    +

    Move from value to decision

    Have each participant connect one confirmed value to a current choice, relationship, responsibility, or opportunity.

    Application questions

    • What decision are you evaluating?
    • Which eternal value should guide it?
    • What option best reflects that value and honors God?

    Commitment: Write one sentence describing how this value will guide your next step.
